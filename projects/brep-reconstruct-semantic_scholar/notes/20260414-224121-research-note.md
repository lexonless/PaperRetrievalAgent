## Key Observations

- Representation strategies are diverse: holistic token sequences (BrepARG), compositional k-cell particles (Flatten The Complex), volumetric distance functions (BR-DF), graph representations (BrepGiff), and local patch encodings (BrepGPT).
- Architectural trends strongly favor autoregressive transformers and diffusion models, often in single-stage frameworks to avoid error accumulation from cascaded pipelines.
- A central challenge is the joint handling of discrete topology and continuous geometry; many papers explicitly decouple these aspects (HiDiGen, DTGBrepGen) or design unified representations that couple them (Flatten The Complex, BrepARG).
- Conditional generation capabilities (from point clouds, images, text) are frequently demonstrated, indicating a move towards practical reconstruction and design applications.
- There is a notable emphasis on ensuring topological validity and watertightness, often through explicit constraint modeling or deterministic post-processing of neural outputs.
