## Key Observations

- Representation Trends: Shift from graph-based or hierarchical representations to unified sequential (token-based) or holistic latent representations that jointly encode geometry and topology.
- Architectural Dominance: Autoregressive Transformers and Diffusion Models are the leading paradigms for direct generation, often combined with techniques like VQ-VAE, flow matching, or Gaussian splatting.
- Task Scope: Methods cover unconditional generation, conditional generation (point clouds, images, text), and reconstruction from sensory data (RGB-D, multi-view).
- Topology-Geometry Coupling: A central challenge; approaches vary from decoupled generation (topology first, then geometry) to joint modeling via shared latents or compositional particles.
- Validity Focus: High emphasis on producing watertight, topologically valid B-Reps, with many papers reporting validity rates as a key metric.

## Open Questions

- How to guarantee 100% topological validity for complex, non-manifold, or high-genus B-Reps?

- What are the limits of sequence-based (autoregressive) generation for very large B-Reps with hundreds of faces?
- How to best integrate differentiable geometric reasoning (e.g., surface intersections, trimming) into end-to-end neural pipelines?
