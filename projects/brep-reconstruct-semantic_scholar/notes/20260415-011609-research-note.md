### 3. B-Rep Distance Functions (BR-DF): How to Represent a B-Rep Model by Volumetric Distance Functions? (2025)

## Key Observations

- The field of direct neural B-Rep generation is highly active from 2024 onward, with a clear trend towards autoregressive Transformer and diffusion models.
- A central challenge is the unified representation of geometry (continuous) and topology (discrete). Core papers propose solutions like holistic token sequences (BrepARG, AutoBrep), distance functions (BR-DF), graph structures (GraphBrep), and unified latent spaces (HoLa).
- Generation is increasingly single-stage and end-to-end, moving away from cascaded pipelines to avoid error accumulation (e.g., BrepGPT, HoLa).
- Topological validity and watertightness are explicit, high-priority goals in recent work.
