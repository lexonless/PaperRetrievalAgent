### 3. AutoBrep: Autoregressive B-Rep Generation with Unified Topology and Geometry (2025)

- **Source:** `arXiv`
- **Link:** http://arxiv.org/abs/2512.03018v1

## Key Observations

- **Major Method Categories:** 1) **Sequence-based Autoregressive (Transformer):** Dominant trend (AutoBrep, BrepGPT, BrepARG). B-Reps are tokenized into sequences (unified or holistic) for next-token prediction. 2) **Diffusion Models:** Used in hierarchical (HiDiGen) or tree-structured (BrepGen) frameworks to denoise geometry and recover topology. 3) **Graph-based Reconstruction:** Earlier approach (ComplexGen) uses GNN/Transformer hybrids to detect primitives and relationships, followed by optimization.
- **Recent Trends:** A clear shift from graph-based representations and multi-stage pipelines towards **unified sequence representations** enabled by transformers. This treats B-Rep generation as a sequence modeling problem, similar to NLP. Another trend is the explicit **decoupling and hierarchical modeling** of topology and geometry (HiDiGen, BrepGen) to manage complexity.
- **Open Challenges:** 1) **Joint Topology-Geometry Validity:** Ensuring generated B-Reps are watertight and topologically sound remains a primary bottleneck. 2) **Scalability to Complexity:** Handling models with high genus, many faces, or free-form surfaces. 3) **Conditional Generation:** Most SOTA methods are unconditional; robust reconstruction from sparse inputs (e.g., single view, partial scan) is less explored. 4) **Evaluation:** Lack of standardized metrics for assessing both topological correctness and geometric fidelity.
